How the test works

  • No lookahead. A signal computed on day t's close fills at day t+1's open. Indicators only see bars up to t.
  • Costs. Commission plus slippage is charged on every entry and exit, in basis points of trade value.
  • Benchmark. Buy & hold enters at the same first open as the strategies and pays one entry cost.
  • Dividends. Adjusted bars correct for splits only, so every line here excludes dividend income.
  • Sharpe. Mean daily return over its standard deviation, times √252, with a 0% risk-free rate.
  • Sample size. A single ticker over a couple of years shows how each rule behaves. It is not evidence that a rule has an edge.
